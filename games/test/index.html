How to Play

Welcome to Rule34dle! Here's how to play:

  • You'll see two characters or tags side by side
  • The left side shows a character/tag with its post count
  • The right side shows another character/tag with a hidden post count (?)
  • Your goal: Click on the character/tag you think has MORE posts
  • If you think the left character has more posts, click on the left card
  • If you think the right character has more posts, click on the right card
  • Try to build the longest streak possible!

Good luck and have fun!

Franchise Filter

What does "Include franchises" mean?

This toggle controls whether the game includes broad franchise tags or focuses on specific characters.

When enabled (default):

  • Shows both individual characters and franchise tags
  • Examples: "Minecraft", "Pokémon", "One Piece"
  • These represent entire franchises with many characters

When disabled:

  • Shows only specific individual characters
  • Examples: "Pikachu", "Princess Peach", "Nami"
  • Filters out the broad franchise categories

Loading screen ahh tooltip: Turn off franchise tags if you prefer comparing individual characters rather than entire franchises!

AI Filter

What does "Remove AI results" mean?

This toggle controls whether the game includes AI-generated posts in the character counts.

When enabled:

  • Shows character post counts with AI posts excluded
  • Only counts human-created artwork and content
  • Provides a more "traditional" view of character popularity

When disabled (default):

  • Shows total post counts including AI-generated content
  • Reflects the current state of the site
  • Includes all types of posts

Toggle this on if you want to see character popularity without AI influence!

Hard Mode

What is Hard Mode?

Hard Mode increases the difficulty by giving you three characters to choose from instead of two, and hiding all post counts!

When enabled:

  • Shows three characters side by side
  • All post counts are hidden
  • Characters are somewhat balanced to avoid extreme differences

We love choosing between three things!

Last Chatter Standing

What is Last Chatter Standing?

An elimination-style game mode where chatters compete until only one remains!

How it works:

  • All chatters start as participants in the first round
  • Vote by typing "1", "2" or "3" (for hard mode) in chat
  • Wrong guesses or failing to vote eliminates you from the game
  • After the first round ends, no new participants can join.
  • Reset the leaderboard to start a new round
  • Eliminated players are greyed out and cannot vote
  • Game continues until 1 player remains or everyone is eliminated

Tip: press the red X on a chatter to eliminate them, and the green button to revive them!
